Behind the architecture

An imposing tower, located near the sea. Designed to make an impact, this tower houses 92 luxury residences, all with 4 suites and at least 3 parking spaces.

Recreation Area

The 5th floor of the tower was exclusively dedicated to a complete and sophisticated leisure area. It encompasses over 2,000m² and includes: party rooms, games room, playroom, baby care, pet care, pet play area, personal care, fitness center, cross-training area, outdoor playground, massage room, sauna, teen space, recreational court, adult pool with a 20m lane, children's pool, fire pit, zen space, and outdoor seating areas.

Rooftop

The 46th floor of the tower boasts an extraordinary rooftop over 150 meters high, providing Dallo Montreux residents with spectacular views of the horizon. The space includes a social area, workspace, and a spacious open-air living room with a fireplace.

Penthouses

The penthouses at Dallo Montreux are beyond exclusive. They are the 14 tallest residences in the tower, each occupying an entire floor. With spacious and integrated areas, they offer their residents the privilege of contemplating the skyline.

Apartments

There are 3 apartments per floor with different layouts, all carefully designed by the Dallo architecture team.
