Inspired by the harmony of French art, the Maison Ladurée tower is an architectural landmark that combines classic and contemporary styles. With its imposing silhouette and timeless details, every line and finish reflects the sophistication that defines the Dallo Standard. Designed to integrate aesthetic beauty and functionality, the tower is an invitation to live at an iconic address in Itapema.

Rising majestically less than 200 meters from the beach, Maison Ladurée is a 40-story tower comprising 44 standard apartments and 10 penthouses, all with 4 suites and at least 3 parking spaces. With panoramic views encompassing the sea, the river, and the entire coastline, all the way to the hills that frame the city, each residence offers a stunning and exclusive setting. Maison Ladurée offers a leisure area distributed across three floors, including an exclusive space on the 6th floor and two rooftop floors. In total, there are more than 1,120 m² dedicated to the well-being and conviviality of the residents.

The tower

Inspired by the harmony of French art, the Maison Ladurée tower is an architectural landmark that combines classic and contemporary styles, rising majestically less than 200 meters from the beach with 40 floors comprising 44 standard apartments and 10 penthouses, all with 4 suites and at least 3 parking spaces.

Rooftop

The tower features two exclusive rooftop floors dedicated to the well-being and social interaction of residents.

Leisure

Maison Ladurée offers a total leisure area spread over three floors, including an exclusive space on the 6th floor and 2 rooftop floors (39th and 40th floors). In total, there are more than 1,120m² dedicated to the well-being and conviviality of residents.

Apartment Types

From the 7th to the 28th floor, the standard apartments at Maison Ladurée offer 173m² of private space and 300m² of total area. They feature 4 suites and 3 parking spaces.

Penthouse

Located between the 29th and 38th floors, the penthouses at Maison Ladurée impress with 302m² of private space and up to 494m² of total area. They feature 4 spacious suites and 3 to 4 parking spaces.
