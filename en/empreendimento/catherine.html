Leisure

Welcome to Dallo Catherine Leisure. 2,488.22 m² distributed across various integrated spaces, designed to provide well-being, health, and fun right at your doorstep.

Rooftop

The 36th floor of the tower was designated for an extraordinary rooftop terrace that is over 130 meters high, providing Dallo Catherine residents with spectacular views of the horizon. The space includes a viewing terrace, a social club, a yoga room, an ecumenical chapel, a workspace, and a gourmet area.

Apartments

Featuring only two residences per floor and a total area of 334 m², the apartments in the Dallo Catherine development are characterized by their exclusivity. Catherine boasts abundant natural light, resulting in dynamic interiors that transform throughout the day.
