The tower

With 60 floors and standing over 220 meters tall, the Dallo Sweden building houses 141 luxury apartments, all with 4 suites and 3 to 5 parking spaces. This imposing tower is located in the center of Itapema, on the city's main avenue, beneath a shopping arcade with 42 stores.

Rooftop

Rooftop Sweden features two entire floors. It offers diverse spaces with fabulous views of the sea and the horizon.
