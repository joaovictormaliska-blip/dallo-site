Our Story

The journey that made us one of the largest construction companies in southern Brazil began in 2008, when brothers Luciano Dallo and Luciel launched their first project in Itapema, Santa Catarina.

Since then, we have strengthened a business focused on civil construction. In 2026, we reached the number of 44 projects launched, with 31 delivered, all on time and with occupancy permits.

We currently have 15 high-end developments under construction simultaneously, which represents more than 1,600 standard Dallo apartments, 1,200 of which have 4 suites.
